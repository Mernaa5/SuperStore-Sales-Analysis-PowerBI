# SuperStore Sales & Profitability Analysis | Power BI

## Project Overview

This project presents an end-to-end Business Intelligence solution developed using Microsoft Power BI to analyze SuperStore's sales performance from 2014 to 2017.

The dashboard transforms raw transactional data into actionable business insights, focusing on sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, product performance, and geographic trends.

## Tools & Technologies

- **Power BI:** Interactive dashboard development and data visualization
- **Power Query:** Data cleaning and transformation
- **DAX:** Calculated measures and KPI development
- **Data Modeling:** Building relationships between datasets
- **Excel:** Data preparation and validation

## Project Workflow

1. **Data Cleaning:** Cleaned and transformed raw data using Power Query.
2. **Data Modeling:** Established relationships between tables to support accurate reporting.
3. **DAX Calculations:** Created measures for sales, profit, profit margin, and customer performance.
4. **Data Analysis:** Analyzed sales trends, customer segments, product categories, and geographic performance.
5. **Dashboard Development:** Designed interactive reports with dynamic filters and visualizations.

## Key Business Insights

- **Sales Growth:** Annual sales increased by approximately 44.5% between 2014 and 2017.
- **Top-Performing Category:** Technology generated approximately $763K in sales.
- **Profitability Gap:** Furniture recorded a profit margin of only 2.36%.
- **Regional Performance:** Texas recorded a loss of approximately $21.31K.
- **Customer Segmentation:** The Home Office segment achieved a profit margin of 14.64%.

## Business Recommendations

Based on the dashboard analysis:

- Review pricing and discount strategies for low-margin product categories.
- Investigate the factors contributing to losses in underperforming states.
- Prioritize high-margin products and profitable customer segments.
- Monitor sales and profitability KPIs to support data-driven business decisions.
